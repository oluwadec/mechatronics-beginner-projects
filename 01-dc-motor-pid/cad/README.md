# CAD Notes — Project 01

## Minimal Mechanical Requirements
- Rigid motor mount so the encoder shaft does not wobble.
- A simple pointer or graduated disc on the output shaft for visual verification.

## Suggested 3D-Printable Parts
1. **Motor clamp** — matches your motor body diameter (common 25 mm or 37 mm).
2. **Base plate** — 80 × 60 × 5 mm with mounting holes for motor clamp and Arduino.
3. **Pointer** — press-fit or set-screw on motor shaft.

## Recommended CAD Tools
- Fusion 360 (free for students/hobbyists)
- Onshape (browser-based)
- FreeCAD or OpenSCAD for parametric designs

## Design Tips
- Add fillets on stress concentrations.
- Leave clearance for motor wires and encoder cable.
- Design the pointer so 0° is visually obvious.

Export STL and print in PLA at 0.2 mm layer height, 20–30% infill.
